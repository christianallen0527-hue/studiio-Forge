# System Overview

Back to [[Auto Edit Forge — HOME]].

## What it is
A **fully automated editor** for YouTube / podcast videos. You give it raw footage from the studio; it produces a finished, polished episode plus vertical shorts — with **no manual editing**.

The benchmark Christian set: **it must look and flow like the most popular 2026 YouTube shows.** The *look* comes from the camera/LUT (not us); the *flow* — pacing, cuts, structure — is our job.

## Philosophy (two things at once)
1. **Fully automated** — Christian should NOT have to do anything for the pipeline to run. Smart defaults, hands-off. Footage in → finished show out.
2. **Fully accessible** — but he wants to *see* the pipeline working and *make changes if he wants*. That's what [[EDITING FORGE UI]] is for: watch it review the clips, watch it forge the edit, tweak options if desired.

## The boundary of its job (critical — see [[Roadmap & Rules]])
- ✅ **DOES:** review footage, remove ums/dead air, tighten pacing, add title/intro/outro, cross-dissolves + fade in/out, **sponsor spots**, audio leveling, make shorts, fix upside-down orientation.
- ❌ **DOES NOT:** color-grade (footage already has a LUT baked in — leave color alone), reframe/crop/punch-in/face-track for framing. A separate **"god-eye" studio-operations system** owns camera work, tracking, framing, and color.
